# Arrive Here

Choose a position that asks as little effort as possible. You can sit, stand, or lie down. If closing your eyes does not feel right, leave them open and let your gaze rest somewhere simple.

[Pause 6 seconds]

Notice that the ground is already meeting you. Feel one or two places where your body is supported: feet on the floor, the seat beneath you, or the surface under your back.

[Pause 8 seconds]

There is nothing special to make happen. Let the next breath arrive in its own shape. Feel where it is easiest to notice. Perhaps at the nose, the chest, or the belly.

[Pause 10 seconds]

When attention moves away, that is not a mistake. The moment you notice is already a moment of returning. Come back to one point of contact, and then to one ordinary breath.

[Pause 12 seconds]

For a little while, receive each inhale. Let each exhale finish without pushing it. Body supported. Breath moving. Sounds coming and going.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Now notice the room around you. Let your eyes take in light and shape if they were lowered. Feel the next small movement you want to make.

You have arrived for these few minutes. Nothing else is required.
