# One Breath at a Time

Settle into a position you can leave or change whenever you need. Let your eyes close only if that feels comfortable; otherwise keep a soft, steady view of the space in front of you. Feel the surface beneath you doing some of the work of holding you.

[Pause 8 seconds]

There is no ideal breath to find. Notice the breath that is already happening. It may be long or short, smooth or uneven, easy to feel or barely noticeable. Let it keep its own pace.

[Pause 10 seconds]

Choose one place where breathing is reasonably clear. You might feel air passing at the nostrils, movement across the chest, or a gentle change around the belly. Stay with just one inhale, from its beginning until it turns.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Then meet one exhale. You do not need to extend it. Notice how it begins, how it moves, and how it comes to an end by itself.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Now receive the next breath in the same way. One inhale. One exhale. This breath does not have to resemble the one before it.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Attention will leave. You may hear a sound, follow a thought, or begin planning. When you notice, there is no need to review where you went. Feel the support beneath you, and meet the breath that is here now.

[Pause 18 seconds]

If attending to breathing feels uncomfortable, move attention to your hands or feet instead. Notice pressure, temperature, or contact. You can let the breath remain in the background and continue with one clear sensation at a time.

[Pause 15 seconds]

For the next few breaths, use a few quiet words if they help: arriving as you breathe in, releasing as you breathe out. Or leave the breath without a label.

[Pause 20 seconds]

Notice that you have not needed to hold every breath in mind. You have only been asked to meet the current one. The practice begins again each time attention returns.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Let awareness widen to include the whole body and the room. Notice light, sound, and the next movement you want to make. Take this simple rhythm with you: not every breath, only the one that is here.
