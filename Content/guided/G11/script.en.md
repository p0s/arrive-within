# Unclench

Pause in a position that is safe and supported. Let the eyes stay open or close. Feel the feet, the seat, or the back meeting a surface.

[Pause 8 seconds]

Notice the hands. Are they holding, pressing, or preparing to act? Without forcing them open, let each finger feel the surface beneath it. Allow the hands to have weight.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Bring attention to the jaw. Notice the tongue and the space between the teeth. If it is comfortable, make one small movement of the jaw and let it find a resting position.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Check the shoulders. Rather than pulling them down, feel where they connect to the arms and upper back. Ask whether they can be carried with slightly less effort.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Take one ordinary breath. A clenched place may remain. Let the nearby body stay available too: face around the jaw, arms around the hands, chest beneath the shoulders.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Notice whether the mind is gripping a sentence, plan, or demand. You do not have to discard it. For one breath, let it wait without being worked on.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Feel hands, jaw, and shoulders together. Keep the effort you need and release only what is ready to go.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Look around the room and make the next movement slowly. You can return to these three places whenever you need a brief check for unnecessary holding.
