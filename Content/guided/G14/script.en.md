# After a Difficult Moment

Pause only where you are physically safe. Keep your eyes open if that feels steadier. You do not need to describe, analyze, or revisit what happened. This practice begins after the difficult moment and stays with the room, the body’s support, and your present choices. Stop or seek help if that is what you need.

[Pause 10 seconds]

Look toward the edges of the space. Find the floor, the ceiling, the nearest wall, and the direction of an exit. Let your head turn slowly if that is comfortable. Notice that this place has shape and boundaries.

[Pause 18 seconds]

Choose one object that feels ordinary: a cup, a door handle, a piece of fabric, a tree, or a patch of light. Notice its color and outline. Let something neutral have your attention for a few breaths.

[Pause 20 seconds]

Listen for the nearest sound. Then the farthest sound you can hear. There may also be quiet between sounds. You do not need to identify their source.

[Pause 20 seconds]

Feel one clear point of support. Press the feet gently into the floor, or let the seat and back meet their surfaces. Notice the response of the surface: solid, soft, warm, cool, still.

[Pause 25 seconds]

Check whether the body wants more or less contact. You might wrap your arms loosely, place a hand on a steady surface, move farther from a doorway, or create more space around you. Choose only what feels appropriate now.

[Pause 25 seconds]

Notice the breath without asking it to calm you. It may be quick, held, uneven, or already settling. Follow one inhale and one exhale as they are. Then return to the support beneath you.

[Pause 25 seconds]

If an image, sentence, or echo from the difficult moment appears, name it simply: a memory is here, or a thought is here. You do not have to enter it. Look again at the ordinary object and notice one new detail.

[Pause 25 seconds]

Let the hands make a small deliberate movement. Open and close the fingers, turn the palms, or touch thumb to fingertip. Feel that the movement begins when you choose and ends when you choose.

[Pause 20 seconds]

Now move the feet or toes. If you are standing, keep your balance and support. Let the body receive a quiet message through movement: this moment includes choice.

[Pause 20 seconds]

Name three present facts. I am in this room. My feet are supported. I can see light. Use facts that are true for you and leave out anything that is not.

[Pause 25 seconds]

Notice whether you need warmth, water, distance, company, quiet, or practical help. The answer may be unclear. You are only checking; you do not have to act before you are ready.

[Pause 25 seconds]

If one next action is obvious and safe, name it in a few words. I will sit here longer. I will message someone. I will step outside. I will return to a familiar task. Keep the action close and concrete.

[Pause 25 seconds]

Feel the whole body in the room. Include the difficult sensations without making them the center. Include clothing, temperature, support, sound, and the space beyond the skin.

[Pause 25 seconds]

Take two ordinary breaths. On each exhale, notice the surface beneath you. On each inhale, let the eyes receive the room. There is no required feeling at the end of this practice.

[Pause 20 seconds]

Look once more toward the exit and toward the place you will move next. Let the movement be slow enough to remain chosen.

The difficult moment does not need to be resolved here. For now, you have returned attention to this place, this support, and the next available step.
