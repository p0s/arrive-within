# Reset Between Tasks

Pause before moving on. Let the previous task be exactly where it is: complete, incomplete, or waiting for someone else. Feel the support beneath your body.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Notice what your hands are doing. Set down anything you do not need for the next minute. Let the eyes look away from the last task and find one neutral shape or color.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Take an ordinary breath in. As you breathe out, allow one small sign of ending: close a window, place a note, or simply say finished for now.

[Pause 15 seconds]

The mind may keep rehearsing what just happened. You do not have to force it silent. Feel the next inhale while the thoughts continue, and feel the next exhale without following them further.

[Pause 18 seconds]

Adjust your posture for what comes next. Perhaps sit back, stand, turn, or let the shoulders move. Choose comfort and alertness rather than a perfect pose.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Name the next task in a few plain words. Then name only its first action. Keep it small enough to begin.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Feel feet, seat, or hands once more. One task can end without being erased. Another can begin without carrying everything at once.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Look toward the first action. When you are ready, move into it at an unhurried pace.
