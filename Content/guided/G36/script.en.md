# Begin Again

Pause in a safe position. Whatever has already happened today does not need to be denied or repaired in these three minutes. Feel one place where the body is supported.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Notice one breath entering and one breath leaving. It can be short, uneven, or quiet. This breath does not have to prove that you are calm.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Perhaps the morning went differently than planned. Perhaps attention wandered, patience ran out, or something remains unfinished. Name only this: that was the earlier part of the day.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Feel the next exhale reach its own end. There is a small space before the next inhale. A new beginning can be this ordinary: not a clean slate, simply another available moment.

[Pause 20 seconds]

Look around and notice three stable things: a color, an edge, a sound, a surface, or the light. Let the present become specific.

[Pause 15 seconds]

Ask what one kind and workable action belongs to the next few minutes. It might be returning, apologizing, resting, asking, drinking water, or beginning one small piece. Choose only one.

[Pause 18 seconds]

Take a natural breath with that choice. You do not need confidence before starting. Feel the body prepare for the first movement.

[Pause 12 seconds]

Begin again from here, bringing the past with honesty and carrying only what the next step needs.
