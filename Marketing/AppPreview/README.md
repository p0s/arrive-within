# App Preview candidate storyboards

The two concepts in `storyboards.json` are production plans, not rendered previews. They deliberately use only real selected-build product surfaces and safe synthetic state. Final capture must preserve the exact source/build identity and use a serialized device or simulator lane.

Before export, reverify Apple's current preview duration, dimensions, frame rate, codec, localization, poster-frame, and upload rules. Record the selected concept, footage hashes, edit manifest, caption sources, objective validation, and human visual/audio review. If neither concept produces a calm, source-faithful result, omit App Preview rather than upload a weak artifact.
