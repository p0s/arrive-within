# Arrive Within visual direction

Status: Active implementation contract

## Visual thesis

A hand-painted, cel-shaded refuge grows from quiet earth into a living twilight sanctuary: tactile, asymmetrical, warm, and recognizably the same tree at every stage, without mystical cliché or game-economy ornament.

## Content plan

1. The Garden is a full-canvas visual anchor centered on one authored tree silhouette.
2. A sparse native overlay communicates journey state and keeps **Meditate** as the single dominant action.
3. Milestone details appear in the world itself—ground, water, light, air, and sky—while the Journey screen carries explanation and variant choice.
4. Native fallback preserves the same state and action hierarchy without imitating an interactive renderer.

## Interaction thesis

- The canopy and atmosphere move slowly enough to feel alive, then pause or reduce rate when attention is elsewhere.
- A qualifying practice reveals deterministic growth over roughly 3–8 seconds; milestone reveals may deepen the composition but remain skippable and exactly replayable.
- A restrained horizontal orbit reveals depth without becoming free-roaming navigation; one reset action always restores the intended composition.
- Reduced Motion keeps camera still and uses a quiet before/after fade instead of spatial or particle-heavy movement.

## Economy rules

- No dashboard mosaic, decorative card stack, currency, inventory, collection reward, avatar, or tap-to-harvest loop.
- One tree, one world, one dominant action, one restrained accent family.
- New milestone geometry must strengthen the tree silhouette or sense of place; it cannot become unrelated prop clutter.
