# Art direction

Arrive Within is a quiet living world centered on one recognizable tree. Growth should make practice emotionally tangible without turning meditation into a game economy.

## Visual meaning

- **Central tree:** continuity, attention, and permanent practice history.
- **Roots and rings:** arrival, grounding, and accumulated care.
- **Threshold/light:** the small transition from outside activity into a practice.
- **Garden:** a responsive environment, never an inventory or collection screen.

The hero tree always has the strongest silhouette, detail, movement, and light. Milestones enrich the same tree and landscape; they do not replace it with a random new tree.

The detailed spatial, architectural, local-time, ecology, and wildlife rules live in the [Garden world contract](../product/GARDEN_WORLD.md). New Garden art must fit that one world rather than add an isolated prop or ambient effect.

## Palette and material

Use deep forest green, misted sage, warm mineral cream, restrained dawn amber, and optional night indigo. Materials are matte, tactile, and softly dimensional. Quantized toon light, colored shadow, restrained ink/outline work, careful antialiasing, and deliberate color grading may support the authored composition.

Keep interfaces quiet and native. Typography, spacing, contrast, and one focal action do more work than decorative chrome. The garden should remain coherent in light/dark contexts, increased contrast, reduced transparency, reduced motion, grayscale, and at low renderer quality.

## Motion

Motion suggests breathing, weather, and growth: slow canopy movement, drifting particles/leaves, water, and restrained camera easing. It must never be required to understand state. Respect Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency; stop rendering in the background. Avoid constant reward bursts, shaking, confetti, streak pressure, or animation that competes with practice.

## Avoid

- photoreal trees, generic stock wellness scenes, or copied reference layouts;
- glassmorphism, gradients, neon game styling, excessive bloom, motion blur, decorative depth of field, or cluttered HUDs;
- lotus flowers, yoga/seated silhouettes, brains, hearts, zen-stone stacks, or a clock as the dominant brand symbol;
- currency, loot, harvesting, tap-to-collect, free-roaming avatars, inventories, or irreversible cosmetic choices;
- Japanese/Sakura identity, SoulGarden/Messenger imagery, or any third-party recognizable composition;
- torii, pagoda tiers, shrine gates, religious statuary, culture-specific sacred ornament, or an indiscriminate mixture of Japanese and Chinese motifs;
- literal reuse of any reference icon’s route, S-curve, arrow, dots, palette, or composition.

## App icon territory

The icon should be one calm singular silhouette: a seed/tree/canopy or a threshold-to-garden gesture. It must read at 40 px, remain distinct in Default/Dark/Tinted appearances, contain no text, and avoid a miniature garden scene. Three preserved concept directions and their provenance live in `docs/brand/provenance/2026-08-10/concept-board/`; none becomes the product icon until the owner records an explicit selection and the final package passes size and compiled/archive inspection.

## Contributing art

Submit source files and deterministic export settings wherever practical. A visual contribution should include:

- the product state or milestone it communicates;
- screenshots at relevant iPhone/iPad sizes and accessibility appearances;
- source, author, license, tool/version, prompts/parameters where generated, and SHA-256 hashes;
- confirmation that no uncertain-rights reference asset or real-person likeness/voice was copied;
- performance impact and low-tier behavior for renderer work.
