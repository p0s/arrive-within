# Product traceability

Status: Active

This public-safe ledger maps stable product identifiers to implementation and evidence. It does not replace the private owner-approved product contract or authorize external actions.

## Personae

- `PERS-001` — Returning beginner: wants a useful, approachable practice without setup or shame.
- `PERS-002` — Independent practitioner: wants precise timer/stopwatch controls, dependable history, and private data ownership.
- `PERS-003` — Overloaded or tired user: needs an appropriate calm practice quickly and safely.
- `PERS-004` — Open-source collaborator: needs the real reproducible product, lawful assets, and evidence-backed contribution rules.
- `PERS-005` — Access-first practitioner: needs the complete product without relying on motion, fine vision, small text, touch precision, or one device shape.
